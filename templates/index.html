What are Review Mismatches?

Our NLP cross-references the assigned star score with the lexical polarity of the customer's text. A 5-star review reporting hardware failure or a 1-star review claiming exceptional performance is automatically isolated as a potential mistake, sarcastic comment, or fraudulent submission.

Key Flag Triggers:
  • ⚠️ 5★ or 4★ rating with negative compound text (≤ -0.05)
  • ⚠️ 1★ or 2★ rating with positive compound text (≥ +0.05)
  • ⚠️ Sarcastic or contradictory purchase headline
